Ho there, young corvid.
You know death, as do I. A force we are called to- a force we respect and admire.
... ... ...
I'm sorry to impart this knowledge unto you, my corvid-kind.
Death grows ambitious. And as you venture eastward, a storm of plague from the west shall follow.
There is no stopping death- but it is senseless for me to remind you of such plain facts, corvus.
The best we can do is warn the living so that they do not find their fate so unfair and ultimate.
Be strong. Fly. May the trinkets, boons, and duty on the path before you be your guide.
