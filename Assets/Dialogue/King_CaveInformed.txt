I see. To these lands you fly to impart upon me a tale of coming plague.
This tale I know. My lord of the mines speaks of rioting earth and a growing rumble. The ground he rules is unstable and prone to change.
My forest lord brings me no such tidings- the last report bore signs of inclement weather, as is common in the wilds.
I appreciate your journey, little raven. Crow. Whatever you may be.
But the sayings from a stranger's beak and exaggerated implications not corroborated elsewhere cannot justify evacuating a kingdom.
I'm sorry, friend. If things are as you say, my eyes will prove the merit of your words before long.
If the inevitability of death is assured, we shall run as you urge us.
