I see. To these lands you fly to impart upon me a tale of coming plague.
This tale I know. My lord of the mines speaks of rioting earth and a growing rumble.
My lord of the forest says the sky darkens and the flowers wilt. 
They have begged for sanctuary, and I have granted it- though I fear knowing what you've seen, and that they corroborate it, means there may be no sanctuary at all.
We are warned thanks to you, small raven. Crow. Whatever you may be.
I merely hope we can outrun death as you have.
