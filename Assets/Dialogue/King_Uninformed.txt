I see. To these lands you fly to impart upon me a tale of coming plague.
This tale, I've yet to hear. My faithful lords of the cave and forest, I trust to bring me such tidings.
From them I have heard only tales of shifting rock as is common in the mines, and inclement weather which the wilds are prone to.
I appreciate your journey, little raven. Crow. Whatever you may be.
But the sayings from a stranger's beak not corroborated elsewhere cannot justify evacuating a kingdom.
I'm sorry, friend. If things are as you say, my eyes will prove the merit of your words before long.
If the inevitability of death is assured, we shall run as you urge us.
